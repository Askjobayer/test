Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide source files (Premiere Pro/After Effects)?
Yes, source files are included in the Premium Package. For Basic or Advance packages, source files can be provided for an additional fee.
How do we transfer large raw footage files?
I prefer using Google Drive, Dropbox, or WeTransfer. Once we start a project, I will share a dedicated secure folder with you to upload your raw footage.
What payment methods do you accept?
I accept Payoneer, Wise, Direct Bank Transfer, and crypto payments for international clients. For local clients, Mobile Banking (bKash/Nagad) is accepted.
What is the usual turnaround time?
Short-form Reels usually take 24-48 hours. Standard 10-minute YouTube videos take 2-4 days. Larger documentaries or heavy VFX projects depend on the complexity.
